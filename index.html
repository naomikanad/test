About us

For students who love to see the world differently.

Our club brings together photographers of all skill levels for photo walks, workshops, portfolio reviews, and student showcases. Whether you shoot with a phone, film camera, or DSLR, you belong here. We welcome complete beginners as well as experienced photographers — many members use only phone cameras, and that's totally fine.
